The Story

"I wanted a place that didn't just have weights, but had a soul of steel. A place where the noise of the world fades, and the only thing left is your heartbeat and the iron."

Founded in 2018 by performance specialist Marcus Vane, House of Gains began as a private garage experiment. Tired of commercial gyms that prioritized memberships over results, Vane built a haven for serious training. Today, that vision has expanded into an 15,000 square foot fortress, serving professional athletes and those who train like them.
